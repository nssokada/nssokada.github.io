The Emory Multimodal Learning Test (EMLT) is a remote memory assessment. A participant works through video-based interactions and answers out loud. Their spoken responses are then scored by a pipeline built on speech recognition and natural language processing.

I led development of the platform as a research assistant with Daniel Drane at Emory University School of Medicine, working on epilepsy research.

## What I built

- The assessment platform: a remote memory assessment using video-based interactions and spoken responses.
- AI scoring: pipelines using speech recognition and natural language processing to evaluate memory responses and candidate digital biomarkers of cognitive impairment.
- The study infrastructure: I coordinated clinicians, researchers, and engineers on clinical study requirements, data infrastructure, automated scoring, and participant-facing tools.

## Piloting and validation

I established partnerships across three clinical institutions to pilot and validate the tool.
