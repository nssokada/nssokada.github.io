How does anxiety change the way people make decisions when they are under threat? As part of my PhD at Caltech with Dean Mobbs, I designed and ran preregistered online behavioral studies with more than 700 participants. I built the experimental platform and developed computational models of decision-making under threat, using hierarchical Bayesian inference and reinforcement learning.
