This project asks whether LLM agents handle shared decisions the way people do. We designed large-scale experiments that put 800 LLM agents in two-party (dyadic) decisions, and compared their coordination and risky decision-making with behavioral data from 764 humans.

The paper, *Do LLM agents compromise like humans? Risk and responsibility in dyadic decision-making*, was accepted to SocialAgent, the Second Workshop on Large Language Models for Social Reasoning and Simulation, at NeurIPS 2026. It is a workshop paper, not a main-conference paper.

## Findings

The agents and the people did not behave the same way. We identified systematic differences in compromise, reciprocity, and how responsibility is attributed.
